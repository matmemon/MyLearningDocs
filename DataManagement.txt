Track E — Data Management
30 topics · 7 parts · Frozen

Out of scope: DBA-level administration, big-data engines such as Spark and Hadoop, BI/reporting tools, ML data pipelines, and message-broker internals.

This track covers application data design, relational and NoSQL stores, data access, consistency, performance, recovery and banking data correctness.

Depth: Master = hands-on; Reason = explain trade-offs; Aware = know it exists.

E6/E8 implementation rule: A transient database failure does not automatically mean the operation is safe to retry. Teach how to identify the complete transaction boundary, how EF Core execution strategies interact with explicit transactions, and how idempotency or reconciliation protects against uncertain commit outcomes.

E30 banking invariant: Each journal transaction must balance according to the ledger's currency and accounting rules. Historical postings should remain immutable; corrections are represented by appropriate reversal and replacement entries rather than silently rewriting the original posting.

Learning rule: For Master topics, build a small .NET data-access application, load realistic data, profile its queries, introduce concurrency and failure, then investigate correctness and performance. For Reason topics, explain consistency, cost, operational and architectural trade-offs. For Aware topics, know the store's purpose and when to evaluate it.

Frozen scope: 30 topics, 7 parts. No E31. Keep DBA administration, big-data platforms and broker internals out of this track.
